# Design Specification

## Canvas

- 1920 × 1080, 16:9.
- Deep navy business style for potential-customer review.

## Palette

- Background: #081526
- Panel: #0F2440
- Border: #173B5F
- Primary text: #F7FBFF
- Secondary text: #AFC3D9
- Blue accent: #37C7FF
- Success green: #2FDB92
- Warning yellow: #FFD166
- Error red: #FF7B7B
- Screenshot background: #FFFFFF

## Typography

- English: Arial with a sans-serif fallback.
- Titles: 54–64px; body: 30–36px; supporting text: 24–28px.
- Title weight: 700; body weight: 400–600.

## Shape and Spacing

- Cards use 24px corners; small labels use 999px corners.
- Page padding is 64px; major gaps are 24–40px.
- Shadows are subtle and used only on screenshot cards.

## Animation

- The title, screenshot, and key points fade in and rise in sequence.
- The bottom progress line grows from left to right over 14 seconds.
- Pages use a 0.5-second crossfade.
- No exaggerated zooms, flashes, rotations, or decorative particles.
